## 1. The one-paragraph version

**A short night does not take a pitcher's fastball. On the best available evidence it takes his strike zone — and the evidence is off-population, so that sentence is EMERGING, not ESTABLISHED.**

Acute sleep loss degrades target-accuracy tasks by roughly three times what it degrades maximal anaerobic power and seven times what it degrades maximal strength. Sleep **extension** above habitual, by contrast, rests on four studies and 57 athletes, three of them uncontrolled single-group pre-post, none in baseball, none with a throwing outcome, and none at low risk of bias. **Protect the floor. Do not sell the ceiling.**

## 2. The numbers worth keeping (all source-verified from Craven 2022, PMC9584849)

Mean %Δ in performance under acute sleep loss, 95% CI. Inclusion required controlled repeated-measures designs; control >6 h/24 h, intervention ≤6 h/24 h; stimulant trials excluded. 227 outcomes, 69 publications, n = 959, 89% male. Search closed **September 2020**.

| Category | Overall | **Early restriction** (late bedtime) | **Late restriction** (early wake) |
|---|---|---|---|
| All categories | −7.56 (−11.9 to −3.13), I²=98.1 | −5.85 (−13.4 to +1.66) **NS** | −7.39 (−10.1 to −4.66) |
| Anaerobic power (58) | −6.26 (−9.10 to −3.41) | **−0.50 (−2.00 to +1.00), I²=0.04** | −7.47 (−11.1 to −3.85) |
| Strength (66) | −2.85 (−4.47 to −1.23) | **−1.16 (−2.57 to +0.25), I²=0.02** | −4.45 (−9.30 to +0.41) |
| Speed/power endurance (32) | −2.90 (−4.97 to −0.82) | +0.49 (−2.05 to +3.04) | −4.38 (−7.15 to −1.62) |
| HIIE (27) | −6.15 (−10.5 to −1.77) | −3.15 (−9.68 to +3.37) | −11.5 (−16.3 to −6.71) |
| Endurance (22) | −5.55 (−8.12 to −2.99) | −5.28 (−9.17 to −1.39) | −3.72 (−6.96 to −0.47) |
| **Skill (13)** | **−20.9 (−27.0 to −14.9), I²=94.1** | **−23.9 (−33.6 to −14.2)** | 1 outcome — unmeasurable |

Skill by test time: AM −14.2 (−26.7 to −1.68); **PM −22.9 (−29.7 to −16.0)**. Skill pool: 9 studies, n = 146, 80% male.

**Meta-regression on hours awake before the task** (%Δ per hour): all categories, restriction **−0.36 (−0.52 to −0.19)**, late restriction **−0.55 (−0.82 to −0.28)**, early restriction −0.10 (−0.27 to +0.09) NS. **Skill: −0.03 (−0.57 to +0.51), p = 0.896 — a flat line.**

### Two warnings that travel with every number above
1. **I² = 98.1 overall, 94.1 on skill.** The pooled estimates locate clouds. Use the sign, not the magnitude.
2. **%Δ is not commensurable across categories** (F-407). Wingate baselines near 700 W; accuracy tasks near 35–70%; Cook's pass plateaus at 9.0/10. A fixed absolute decrement produces a much bigger %Δ on the accuracy task. **The direction survives this objection; the 20.9 : 6.26 ratio does not.**

## 3. The task list behind "skill"

Craven's own category definition: *"Task that requires high cognitive component for execution — Tennis serving; rugby passing; free-throw shooting (basketball); shooting."* Identified studies in the pool include:

- **Edwards & Waterhouse 2009** — *dart throwing*, one night partial deprivation, *Chronobiol Int* 26(4):756–768
- **Reyner & Horne 2013** — tennis serving (5 h restriction, 40 serves into a 1.8 × 1.1 m box, semi-pro players, double-blind 80 mg caffeine arm), *Physiol Behav* 120:93–96 — ⚠️ SNIPPET-ONLY, magnitude not reliably extractable
- **Cook 2011** — rugby passing through a 1.5 m hoop, elite professionals — read in full, see §4
- **Filipas 2021** — basketball free throw, acute sleep restriction + mental fatigue, *IJSPP* 16(3):415–420
- **Froberg 1975** — rifle shooting under sleep deprivation, *Biol Psychol* 2:175–188

**This list is why the sleep literature's command-relevant half is its useful half (§6).** Darts, serves, passes, free throws and rifle shots are target accuracy. They need no transfer step to reach a pitching coach.

## 4. Cook 2011 — the best population match in the topic

n = 10 professional rugby backs, **age 20 ± 0.5 y, 90 ± 4 kg, 1.81 m**. Within-subject, randomized, placebo-controlled. Ten sessions over ten weeks, ≥3 days apart, **all at 1130 h**. Sleep: 7–9 h × 2 nights before five tests; **3–5 h on the single night before the other five**.

**Result:** sleep deprivation with placebo → significant accuracy fall, **both sides, p < 0.001**. Caffeine (1, 5 mg/kg) and creatine (50, 100 mg/kg) all significantly better than placebo, p < 0.001; sleep × treatment interactions significant.

⚠️ **THE MAGNITUDE IS FIGURE-ONLY AND UNRETRIEVABLE.** Do not import a number for it.
⚠️ **Whether caffeine helped RESTED athletes cannot be resolved from the text** — the difference between "rescue" and "command drug."

### Table 1 — the familiarisation curve, and the most portable result in the cycle

| | 1st trial | 5th trial | 12th trial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dominant | 7.3 ± 0.8 | **9.0 ± 0.7** | 9.0 ± 0.4 |
| Non-dominant | 5.7 ± 0.8 | **8.3 ± 0.8** | 8.2 ± 0.7 |

*"Skill execution on both sides improved significantly (p < 0.001) across the first 5 sessions and then was unchanged between session 5 and 12."* Within-individual variability on rested days <5%; between individuals <15%. Placebo on rested days did not differ from the last familiarisation trial (F(1,36) = 0.00, p = 1.0).

**Use it as an instrument (F-404):** in *professionals*, a *simple* accuracy task took **five sessions** and **+1.7 to +2.6 points out of 10** to plateau. Any pre/post accuracy study with fewer than five familiarisation sessions and no control group is measuring learning.

## 5. The extension literature, in full

| Study | Sport | n | Design | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Roberts et al. | Cycling/triathlon | 9 | **Counterbalanced crossover** | PVT; endurance TT |
| Schwartz & Simon 2015 | Tennis | 12 (42% male) | Pre-post, one group, **diary only** | Serving accuracy |
| Swinbourne 2018 | Rugby | 25 | Pre-post, one group | 5-min PVT |
| Mah 2011 | Basketball | 11 | Pre-post, one group | Sprint, FT%, 3P% |

**Four studies. 57 athletes. Three uncontrolled. Zero baseball. Zero throwing outcomes.**
Cunha 2023, verbatim: *"the strengths of our conclusions are limited, since none of the included studies presented a low risk of bias."* Under RoB2: 5 high risk, 10 some concerns, 0 low.

### Swinbourne 2018 read at source — the compliance number

Target **10 h**. Achieved (actigraphy, n = 18):

| | Control | Sleep extension |
|---|---|---|
| Time in bed | 516 → 488 min | 529 → **549 min (9.1 h)** |
| **Total sleep time** | 391 → **382 min (6.4 h)** | 422 → **441 min (7.4 h)** |
| Sleep efficiency | 75.0 → 79.4% | 79.9 → 80.8% |

**A ten-hour prescription produced 7 h 24 min, from 9.1 hours in bed at ~80% efficiency. Every hour of sleep costs ~75 minutes of schedule.** Write the policy against time in bed, not against the target.

**Three problems found by reading it** (F-405): the reaction-time result is pre-post **inside the intervention arm only** with no control comparison; **Table 5 reports n = 27 in a study that enrolled 25**; and "Slowest 10% time (ms): 3.0 → 3.3" is not milliseconds. Inference throughout is magnitude-based at 90% limits (e.g. −24.8%; **±54.1%**, reported as "moderate").

## 6. The structural argument (F-406) — why the command claim beats the velocity claim

The sleep literature reports two kinds of outcome.

**Power outcomes** (jump, sprint, Wingate) must pass a transfer step to reach a radar gun. This corpus has already priced it:
- **F-004** — vertical jump height vs fastball velocity, **r ≈ 0.07**
- **F-005** — relative CMJ power r = 0.19 NS; absolute power r = 0.43
- **F-359** — **117 maximum-effort pitches moved ball velocity and hip strength and moved no squat-jump variable**

**Skill outcomes** (darts, serves, passes, free throws) need no transfer step. They *are* target accuracy.

**And the larger effect is on the side that needs no transfer: skill −20.9% vs power −6.26%.**

**The claim is comparative, and must be stated that way.** The command claim is one unverified step from the data — does a rugby pass generalise to a slider? The velocity claim is two — the same construct leap **plus** a transfer coefficient that has been measured and failed.

⚠️ **The coach's objection was conceded and stands on the record:** the construct match between "target accuracy" and "command" is an argument, not a measurement, and this corpus killed stride length and extension for exactly that error. **§7 is the falsification test.**

## 7. What to do, and how to check it

### The recommendation: a WAKE-TIME floor, not a bedtime rule
1. Late restriction (early wake) is where the power cost sits: **−7.47% vs −0.50%**.
2. Hours awake at first pitch scales the decrement (**−0.36%/h**, restriction).
3. **A wake time is enforceable by a schedule. A bedtime is not.**

**Concrete:** nothing on the schedule before ~9:30 a.m. on the day of a night start; move the lift, don't move the bedtime.

⚠️ **NEVER convert the −0.36%/h into mph, and never say it to an athlete.** §6 explains why that number cannot reach a radar gun. **The reason to do this is that it is free, not that the effect is big.**

⚠️ **And never tell a pitcher a late night is free.** It is free for peak output only. The same protocol produced the **largest** accuracy decrement in the meta-analysis (−23.9%).

### The check (F-408)

| Design | Outcome | n per condition | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Binary, % inside a 10-in circle (40% → 32%) | **~563 pitches** | 45 blocks of 25 — **unaffordable for one arm** |
| **B** | **Location SD, 20% increase** | **~118 pitches** | **Five 25-pitch bullpens per condition — run this one** |
| B′ | Location SD, 10% increase | ~431 pitches | Declare which you're testing before you start |
| C | Binary, pooled across 12 pitchers | ~47 each | ~3 weeks, but between-athlete confounds |

⚠️ **The powering assumption is the corpus's own inference:** that a 20% skill decrement corresponds to a 20% increase in location SD. **Nobody has measured that correspondence in any sport.**

**Three instrument warnings:**
1. **≥5 familiarisation sessions before the first scored block** (F-404).
2. **No jump mat as the readiness instrument** (F-359).
3. **No zone rate** — catcher-contaminated (F-309), ~2,500 taken pitches per catcher to resolve half an inch (F-310). **Location and release-point SD, seventh time of asking.**

**Run Design B as a falsification test of §6, not a confirmation of it. If location SD does not widen after a short night, F-406 is wrong and the corpus says so.**

## 8. Circadian timing — filed, not actionable

**Chronotype × start time in MLB pitchers** (2010 SLEEP-meeting presentation, ⚠️ SNIPPET-ONLY): day games morning types **3.06** vs evening types **3.49** ERA; night games evening types **4.07** vs morning types **4.15**. The crossover form is right; **the two halves are 0.43 and 0.08**, so it is carried almost entirely by one cell. No n, no CI. **WEAK.**

**Jet lag** (Song, Severini & Allada 2017, *PNAS* 114(6):1407–1412, ⚠️ SNIPPET-ONLY): eastward travel degraded performance for home and away teams **specifically through pitchers allowing more home runs**. **Formally contested in the same journal** — Joly, *PNAS* 114(16):E3168, "Correlation does not imply causation," noting **the word "correlation" does not appear once in the original**; reply at 114(16), "Inferring causation from comprehensive analysis of observation."

**Keep the exchange, not the number.** It is the corpus's founding error committed in public by a top-journal paper, and named out loud by a peer. **The SEC is one time zone wide; the travel magnitudes do not transfer.**

## 9. Gaps this topic opens

1. **No sleep manipulation has ever been run on a pitcher with a pitching outcome.** Not one. (F-409)
2. **NCT07212101** — Columbia University, sleep + diet intervention, **pitchers' throwing velocity by radar gun**, started 2025-09-16, **listed COMPLETED 2025-10-31**, no publication located. ⚠️ SNIPPET-ONLY; clinicaltrials.gov is egress-blocked. **Top of the verification queue, and asymmetrically informative: a null strengthens §6, a velocity gain refutes it.**
3. **PMID 39893113** — *Sleep Health* 2025, 24 D-I baseball players, weekly peak power × 12 weeks, Oura × 6 weeks (Scullin lab, Baylor). **Best-matched sample in the topic; measures the one variable F-004 and F-359 say is blind to pitching.** Result unretrieved.
4. **No measured correspondence between a skill-accuracy decrement and pitch-location SD**, in any sport. This is what F-408's powering rests on.
5. **Zero data on sleep and pitch-to-pitch variability**, which is the outcome §6 predicts and nobody has measured.
6. **The within-session decay Cook observed** — accuracy falling in the second 10 of 20 reps under sleep loss — has never been tested across an outing. It predicts a sixth-inning command problem, not a first-inning one.
